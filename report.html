What was tested

  • Live data (18 Nov 2026): 9 chains' branch locations in Hamburg from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim); distance from each postcode centre to the nearest branch of each chain. Rewe online-shop prices were checked live at 3 postcodes (20457 HafenCity, 21073 Harburg, 22767 Altona): all 11 Rewe products were in stock at identical prices. 22143 Rahlstedt could not be selected in Rewe's postcode dialog. Prices on dm, Aldi Nord and Action were re-read from the store sites and were unchanged. Aldi's Weihnachtsmann offers end 21 Nov, and the app now marks them as ended and drops them after that date automatically.
  • Scenarios per postcode: S1 20 gifts/€30 · S2 20/€20 · S3 10/€15 · S4 20/€10 (tight) · S5 20/€1 (impossible) · S6 5/€10 + gift bags · S7 30/€40.
  • Checks on every combination returned: exactly N gifts · total ≤ budget · only stores within 2 km used · ≤ 3 of any product · store subtotals add up · no NaN · "Cheapest" tab really is the cheapest · cheapest result within €0.60 of the theoretical minimum · postcode auto-selects exactly the stores in the distance table.
  • Impossible cases: the app's verdict is compared against an independent calculation of the cheapest possible list. Each fix button (raise budget, fewer gifts, allow identical) must lead to a working result, and a suggested budget must be higher than the current one.
  • Languages: every English text has a German translation, and no raw text keys show in either language. No JavaScript errors.

Bugs found and fixed during this run

  1. Postcode lookup: the first geocoding pass placed all 50 postcodes at Hamburg's city centre. Fixed by querying by postcode only, and re-verified (Altstadt, Eimsbüttel, Harburg… now distinct).
  2. Search engine: 20 gifts for €10 was reported as impossible even though a €9.62 list exists, because the look-ahead ignored the "max 3 identical" rule. Fixed with a limit-aware look-ahead plus a guaranteed cheapest-first fallback.
  3. Impossible-case advice: when only the identical-gift limit blocked a list, the app suggested a lower budget, and "make it N gifts" ignored the limit. Both are fixed and covered by tests.
